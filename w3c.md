## Introduction

“Linking” - Is the key development trait that makes the web and mobile technology, seamless. There are various powerful types of linking present today.

The one linking I missed was where I can browse the web while separating my project work from one to the next. Today the internet is unified, everything belongs to one environment and thinking space.

I have observed about myself and generally assumed is that we can carry some memory of the contexts that make up our daily lives. We can’t remember too many distinct occurrences of the same object, but we surely can manage few.

Thus the internet being just one object or thinking space is currently underusing the power of my brain. That is all what I want to assert. 

Hence, I am proposing that the internet be segregated to use my powerful brain in the occupation of linking whatever I browse into separate projects. Two are possible, any number is useful. If I mange two, I have at-least two different projects I can work on. If I have 100, the previous ones are forgotten and become versions of how I used to think in the past.
